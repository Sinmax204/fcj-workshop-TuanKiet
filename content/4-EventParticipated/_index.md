# Events Participated

During my internship, I had the opportunity to participate in two AWS community events. These workshops not only expanded my knowledge of cloud computing and Generative AI but also allowed me to interact with AWS experts, industry professionals, and other cloud enthusiasts.

The events helped me gain practical insights into modern application architecture, serverless computing, AI-powered development, and cloud-native best practices. They also strengthened my communication, networking, and continuous learning skills, which are essential for a career in cloud computing.

---

## [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)

**Event Name:** AWS Student Builder Group Meetup – Build Voice Agent

**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, May 30, 2026

**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Overview:**

This meetup introduced participants to modern Voice AI technologies and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. The speakers shared practical approaches to building AI-powered voice applications using AWS serverless services and demonstrated how AI Agents can be integrated into real-world business solutions.

---

## [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)

**Event Name:** GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization Workshop

**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026

**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Overview:**

This workshop focused on modernizing applications and databases using AWS cloud services and Generative AI. Topics included Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Event-Driven Architecture, serverless computing, and Amazon Q Developer. The workshop provided practical guidance on designing scalable cloud-native applications and modern software development practices.

---

Overall, participating in these two events gave me valuable opportunities to enhance both my technical knowledge and professional skills. The experience reinforced many concepts that I had learned during the internship while also exposing me to the latest AWS technologies and industry best practices.
